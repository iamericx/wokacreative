THE SHORT VERSION

Design caught my eye.
Building kept me curious.

After finishing my studies, I went looking for a creative path and found graphic design. I learned to pay attention to composition, color, and typography — the small decisions that make a visual idea communicate.

Before long, I wanted to see those ideas live on screen. So I taught myself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and began bringing my designs to life in the browser.

These days, I work across both sides of that process: shaping a brand’s visual identity, then carrying it through into a clear, responsive website.
